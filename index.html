About

I am Mo Christensen, and I run The Parallel Office. I build the behind-the-scenes systems that help local businesses get found and get called: complete Google profiles, follow-up systems that do not drop leads, and booking that runs itself.

Most customers meet your Google profile long before they meet your website or your truck. They search a trade, see a few businesses on a map, and call the one that looks complete, current, and alive. My job is to make sure that business is yours.

The Cleanup fixes your whole profile in one go, without a new website, an ad budget, or a marketing degree.

  • A baseline snapshot of where your profile stands now, plus a look at your top 3 competitors
